Third-Party Travel Service Provider Agreement

Effective Date: July 21, 2026

This Third-Party Travel Service Provider Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Sri Lanka Tour Pilot ("Platform", "Sri Lanka Tour Pilot", "we", "our", or "us") and the registered third-party travel service provider ("Service Provider", "Provider", "you", or "your").

By registering, listing services, accepting bookings, or providing services through SriLankaTourPilot.com, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Agreement.

1. Purpose and Scope

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot is an online travel marketplace that connects travelers with tourism-related service providers, including but not limited to:

Hotels and accommodation providers
Villas, resorts, and homestays
Transportation providers
Chauffeur and driver services
Safari operators
Activity and excursion providers
Licensed tour guides
Restaurants and dining partners
Attraction and experience providers
Wellness and leisure service providers
Other tourism-related suppliers

This Agreement defines the terms and conditions under which Service Providers may offer services through Sri Lanka Tour Pilot.

2. Independent Service Provider Relationship

The Service Provider acknowledges and agrees that:

It operates as an independent business entity.
Sri Lanka Tour Pilot acts as a technology platform and marketplace facilitator.
No partnership, employment, agency, joint venture, or franchise relationship is created between Sri Lanka Tour Pilot and the Service Provider unless separately agreed in writing.
The Service Provider remains solely responsible for delivering the services offered to travelers.
3. Eligibility and Registration Requirements

To become a registered Service Provider, you must:

Be legally authorized to provide your services.
Provide accurate business and contact information.
Maintain all required licenses, permits, registrations, and approvals.
Comply with applicable Sri Lankan laws and regulations.
Provide verification documents requested by Sri Lanka Tour Pilot.
Maintain appropriate insurance coverage where applicable.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot reserves the right to verify information and reject or suspend registrations that do not meet platform standards.

4. Service Listings and Information Accuracy

The Service Provider is responsible for ensuring that all information provided to Sri Lanka Tour Pilot is accurate, complete, and updated.

This includes:

Service descriptions
Pricing
Availability
Photos and videos
Locations
Operating hours
Inclusions and exclusions
Terms and conditions
Safety information
Cancellation policies
Special requirements

The Service Provider shall not publish misleading, inaccurate, exaggerated, or incomplete information.

5. Service Quality Standards

The Service Provider agrees to:

Deliver services exactly as advertised.
Maintain professional service standards.
Provide safe, clean, and suitable facilities, equipment, vehicles, and accommodation where applicable.
Ensure employees, guides, drivers, and representatives are properly trained and qualified.
Treat all travelers respectfully and professionally.
Maintain compliance with health, safety, and industry standards.

Any significant change to the advertised service must be communicated to Sri Lanka Tour Pilot before affecting customer bookings.

6. Pricing and Platform Pricing Rights

The Service Provider shall provide accurate base pricing for services listed on Sri Lanka Tour Pilot.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot reserves the right to manage customer-facing pricing displayed on the Platform.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may:

Add platform service fees or booking charges.
Apply discounts, promotional offers, coupons, gift vouchers, affiliate benefits, and loyalty rewards.
Adjust customer-facing prices for marketing campaigns, seasonal promotions, competitive positioning, or commercial purposes.

Such pricing adjustments shall not change the agreed payout amount, commission structure, or commercial terms with the Service Provider unless otherwise agreed.

The Service Provider shall not offer conflicting prices to customers outside the Platform where such action violates Sri Lanka Tour Pilot policies.

7. Booking Acceptance and Fulfillment

The Service Provider agrees to:

Accept and honor confirmed bookings.
Maintain accurate availability.
Provide timely booking confirmations.
Deliver services according to the confirmed booking details.
Inform Sri Lanka Tour Pilot immediately of any inability to fulfill a booking.

Repeated failures to honor confirmed bookings may result in penalties, suspension, or removal from the Platform.

8. Cancellations, Refunds, and Changes

The Service Provider shall comply with the cancellation and refund conditions displayed at the time of booking.

If the Service Provider cancels a confirmed booking without a valid reason:

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may:

Arrange alternative services.
Issue refunds to travelers.
Recover related costs from amounts payable to the Service Provider.
Suspend or terminate the Service Provider account.

For circumstances beyond the Service Provider's control, Sri Lanka Tour Pilot will work with the Provider and traveler to find a reasonable solution.

9. Communication Requirements

The Service Provider shall maintain professional communication with all parties, including:

Travelers and guests
Tour Agents
Affiliates
Hotels and accommodation partners
Transportation providers
Activity operators
Guides
Sri Lanka Tour Pilot staff
Other marketplace participants

The Service Provider shall not:

Harass, threaten, or abuse any person.
Provide misleading information.
Attempt to bypass Sri Lanka Tour Pilot for bookings made through the Platform.
Request unauthorized direct payments from customers.
Use traveler information for unrelated marketing without consent.
Share confidential information without authorization.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may monitor communications conducted through the Platform for quality control, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and compliance purposes.

10. Customer Data Protection

The Service Provider may receive traveler information only for fulfilling confirmed services.

The Service Provider agrees to:

Protect customer personal information.
Use data only for legitimate service delivery purposes.
Follow applicable privacy and data protection requirements.
Not sell, distribute, or misuse customer information.
11. Promotions, Discounts, and Gift Vouchers

The Service Provider agrees to honor approved:

Promotional campaigns
Discount coupons
Gift vouchers
Seasonal offers
Affiliate promotions
Special packages

created or approved through Sri Lanka Tour Pilot.

Any restrictions, exclusions, or validity conditions must be clearly communicated and approved before publication.

Customers may provide reviews and ratings based on their experience.

The Service Provider agrees:

Not to create fake reviews.
Not to manipulate ratings.
Not to pressure customers for misleading reviews.
To respond professionally to feedback.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may remove reviews that violate platform policies.

Promotional activities

Sri Lankan tourism regulations
Licensing requirements
Environmental regulations
Safety standards
Consumer protection laws
Tax obligations
Employment laws

The Service Provider is solely responsible for maintaining required approvals.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may suspend or terminate access where the Service Provider:

Provides unsafe or poor-quality services.
Violates this Agreement.
Provides inaccurate information.

Engages in fraudulent activity.

20. Amendments
