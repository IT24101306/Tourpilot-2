1. About Sri Lanka Tour Pilot

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot is an online travel marketplace that connects travelers with independent Tour Agents offering tours, transportation, accommodations, activities, and other tourism-related services throughout Sri Lanka.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot acts as a technology platform facilitating bookings between travelers and Tour Agents. Tour services are provided by the respective Tour Agents.
