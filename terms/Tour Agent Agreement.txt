Tour Agent Agreement

Effective Date: July 21, 2026

This Tour Agent Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Sri Lanka Tour Pilot ("Platform", "we", "our", or "us") and the registered Tour Agent ("Tour Agent", "Agent", "you", or "your").

By registering as a Tour Agent or offering tours and travel services through SriLankaTourPilot.com, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Agreement.

1. Purpose

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot is an online travel marketplace that connects travelers with independent Tour Agents offering tours, transportation, accommodation arrangements, activities, and other travel-related services.

This Agreement sets out the rights, responsibilities, and obligations of Tour Agents using the Platform.

2. Eligibility

To register as a Tour Agent, you must:

Be at least 18 years of age.
Have the legal authority to enter into this Agreement.
Provide accurate and complete registration information.
Hold all licenses, permits, registrations, and approvals required to legally operate your tourism business.
Maintain any insurance required under applicable laws or industry standards.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot reserves the right to request supporting documentation at any time.

3. Account Registration

The Tour Agent agrees to:

Maintain accurate business information.
Keep contact details up to date.
Protect account credentials.
Notify Sri Lanka Tour Pilot immediately of any unauthorized account access.

The Tour Agent is responsible for all activities carried out using their account.

4. Tour Listings

The Tour Agent is responsible for ensuring that all listings are accurate, complete, and up to date, including:

Tour descriptions
Prices
Inclusions and exclusions
Duration
Pickup and drop-off details
Availability
Images
Safety information
Cancellation policy
Special requirements

Misleading or false information is strictly prohibited.

4A. Package Management Rights

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot operates as a travel marketplace platform and reserves the right to manage, optimize, and maintain the quality of tour packages, services, and offerings available through the Platform.

The Tour Agent acknowledges and agrees that Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may, at its discretion:

Add new packages, tours, activities, or service offerings created in collaboration with Tour Agents.
Modify existing packages, including package names, descriptions, inclusions, exclusions, pricing presentation, images, marketing content, or other customer-facing information.
Remove, deactivate, or discontinue any package, listing, or service from the Platform.
Combine, restructure, categorize, or promote packages to improve customer experience, marketplace performance, or operational efficiency.
Create customized packages, promotional packages, seasonal packages, affiliate packages, or marketplace offers based on services provided by the Tour Agent.

Where such changes materially affect the Tour Agent's services, commercial arrangements, or ongoing commitments, Sri Lanka Tour Pilot will provide the Tour Agent with a minimum of three (3) months prior written notice before implementing such changes.

The Tour Agent agrees to cooperate with Sri Lanka Tour Pilot regarding package updates, content improvements, pricing adjustments, and service enhancements required for maintaining the quality and competitiveness of the Platform.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot's rights under this section do not remove the Tour Agent's responsibility to deliver confirmed bookings that were accepted before any package modification, suspension, or removal takes effect.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot retains the final authority over the structure, presentation, availability, and organization of packages displayed on the Platform.

5. Pricing and Platform Pricing Rights

The Tour Agent is responsible for providing accurate base pricing for all tours, packages, activities, and services listed on Sri Lanka Tour Pilot.

The Tour Agent shall:

Maintain accurate and updated pricing information.
Honor the agreed base prices provided to Sri Lanka Tour Pilot for confirmed bookings.
Clearly communicate any changes to pricing, inclusions, exclusions, or additional charges before such changes are applied.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot reserves the right to determine, adjust, and manage the final selling price displayed to travelers ("Customer Price") on the Platform.

The Tour Agent acknowledges and agrees that Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may, at its discretion:

Add platform service fees, booking fees, convenience fees, promotional adjustments, or other applicable charges.
Apply discounts, promotional campaigns, coupons, gift vouchers, affiliate commissions, loyalty benefits, or marketing incentives.
Modify customer-facing prices to support marketing strategies, competitive positioning, seasonal campaigns, or business requirements.
Offer special rates or packages to travelers, affiliates, corporate partners, or promotional campaigns.

Any changes made by Sri Lanka Tour Pilot to the customer-facing price shall not alter the agreed Tour Agent payout amount, commission structure, or commercial terms unless otherwise agreed between Sri Lanka Tour Pilot and the Tour Agent.

The Tour Agent shall not directly communicate or advertise alternative prices to customers that conflict with the pricing displayed through Sri Lanka Tour Pilot.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot retains full control over pricing strategies, promotions, and customer offers presented through its Platform.

6. Booking Management

The Tour Agent agrees to:

Respond promptly to booking requests.
Honor confirmed reservations.
Maintain accurate availability calendars.
Communicate promptly regarding operational changes.
Provide travelers with all necessary pre-arrival information.

Repeated booking cancellations or poor response times may result in account suspension.

7. Service Standards

The Tour Agent shall:

Deliver all services as advertised and in accordance with the itinerary, inclusions, pricing, and conditions presented on the Platform at the time of booking.
Honor all confirmed bookings, promotional offers, discounts, discount coupons, gift vouchers, special packages, and other marketing campaigns published or approved through Sri Lanka Tour Pilot, unless otherwise stated in the applicable terms and conditions.
Ensure that any limitations, exclusions, blackout dates, or conditions applicable to promotions, discounts, coupons, or gift vouchers are clearly disclosed before a traveler completes a booking.
Treat all travelers professionally, courteously, and without discrimination.
Maintain high standards of customer service throughout the booking and travel experience.
Provide clean, safe, and suitable vehicles, accommodation arrangements, equipment, and facilities where applicable.
Employ qualified guides, drivers, and other personnel with the necessary licenses, certifications, and experience required to deliver the services offered.
Comply with all applicable health, safety, environmental, and tourism regulations.
Promptly notify Sri Lanka Tour Pilot of any circumstances that may affect the delivery of booked services, including delays, itinerary changes, cancellations, safety concerns, or force majeure events.

8. Legal Compliance

The Tour Agent warrants that it complies with all applicable laws and regulations, including those relating to:

Tourism operations
Consumer protection
Employment
Taxation
Road transport
Wildlife and environmental protection
Occupational health and safety
Licensing and permits

The Tour Agent is solely responsible for obtaining and maintaining all necessary approvals required to provide its services.

9. Payments and Commissions

Bookings made through Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may be subject to platform commissions or service fees as agreed between the parties.

Unless otherwise agreed:

Payments will be processed through the Platform or an approved payment provider.
Payouts to the Tour Agent will be made according to the agreed payment schedule.
Applicable taxes remain the responsibility of the Tour Agent.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot reserves the right to deduct agreed commissions, applicable fees, refunds, chargebacks, or other amounts due before releasing payments.

10. Cancellations and Refunds

The Tour Agent shall clearly specify cancellation terms for each tour listing.

Where a booking is cancelled:

Refunds shall be processed in accordance with the published cancellation policy.
The Tour Agent agrees to cooperate with Sri Lanka Tour Pilot in resolving refund requests fairly and promptly.

If the Tour Agent cancels a confirmed booking without a valid reason, Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may:

Issue a full refund to the traveler.
Apply financial penalties where agreed.
Temporarily suspend listings.
Terminate the Tour Agent's account for repeated violations.

11. Communications and Platform Conduct

The Tour Agent agrees to communicate with all parties in a professional, respectful, timely, and lawful manner.

This obligation applies to communications with, including but not limited to:

Travelers and guests
Prospective customers
Affiliates and referral partners
Third-party travel service providers
Hotels and accommodation providers
Transportation providers
Activity and excursion operators
Licensed guides
Restaurants and other tourism suppliers
Sri Lanka Tour Pilot administrators and support staff

The Tour Agent shall not:

Harass, threaten, intimidate, or abuse any individual or organization.
Use offensive, defamatory, discriminatory, or inappropriate language.
Misrepresent services, pricing, availability, or booking conditions.
Request, encourage, or accept payments outside the Platform where prohibited by Sri Lanka Tour Pilot policies.
Encourage travelers, affiliates, or other partners to bypass the Platform for confirmed or future bookings where prohibited by Platform policies.
Share confidential business information or personal data without proper authorization.
Engage in spam, unsolicited marketing, fraudulent communications, or any activity that may damage the reputation of Sri Lanka Tour Pilot or its partners.

The Tour Agent acknowledges that all communications conducted through the Platform may be monitored, retained, or reviewed by Sri Lanka Tour Pilot for quality assurance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes, in accordance with the Platform's Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

12. Reviews and Ratings

Travelers may leave reviews based on their experience.

The Tour Agent agrees:

Not to manipulate reviews.
Not to submit fake reviews.
Not to offer incentives in exchange for positive reviews unless expressly permitted by the Platform.
To respond respectfully to customer feedback.

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot reserves the right to remove reviews that violate its policies.

13. Data Protection and Privacy

The Tour Agent shall:

Use traveler information only for the purpose of fulfilling confirmed bookings.
Protect personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
Comply with applicable data protection and privacy laws.
Not sell, distribute, or use traveler information for unrelated marketing without the traveler's consent.
14. Intellectual Property

The Tour Agent retains ownership of its trademarks, logos, and original content.

By publishing content on Sri Lanka Tour Pilot, the Tour Agent grants the Platform a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, display, translate, adapt, and promote such content for the purpose of operating and marketing the Platform.

The Tour Agent warrants that it owns or has permission to use all submitted content.

15. Prohibited Activities

The Tour Agent shall not:

Publish false or misleading information.
Engage in fraudulent activity.
Infringe intellectual property rights.
Manipulate pricing or availability.
Discriminate against travelers.
Use the Platform for unlawful purposes.
Upload malicious software or harmful content.
Interfere with the operation or security of the Platform.
16. Suspension and Termination

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may suspend or terminate a Tour Agent account where the Tour Agent:

Breaches this Agreement.
Receives repeated verified complaints.
Provides unsafe services.
Fails to comply with legal requirements.
Engages in fraudulent or deceptive conduct.
Damages the reputation of the Platform.

The Tour Agent may terminate this Agreement by providing written notice, subject to fulfilling all outstanding booking obligations.

17. Limitation of Liability

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot provides a technology platform that facilitates connections between travelers and Tour Agents.

Except where required by law, Sri Lanka Tour Pilot is not responsible for:

The quality or performance of tour services.
Personal injury or property damage arising from services provided by the Tour Agent.
Delays caused by weather, traffic, natural disasters, government actions, or other circumstances beyond its reasonable control.
Acts or omissions of the Tour Agent, its employees, subcontractors, or suppliers.

The Tour Agent remains solely responsible for the services it provides.

18. Indemnification

The Tour Agent agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Sri Lanka Tour Pilot, its directors, employees, affiliates, and partners against any claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, or expenses arising from:

The Tour Agent's services.
Breach of this Agreement.
Violation of applicable laws.
Negligence or misconduct.
Infringement of third-party rights.
19. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures to perform resulting from events beyond their reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, civil unrest, government restrictions, strikes, or severe weather conditions.

The affected party shall notify the other party as soon as reasonably practicable.

20. Amendments

Sri Lanka Tour Pilot may update this Agreement from time to time.

Updated versions will be published on the Platform with a revised effective date. Continued use of the Platform after such updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Agreement.

21. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiations. If a dispute cannot be resolved amicably, it shall be submitted to the competent courts of Sri Lanka, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

22. Contact Information

Questions regarding this Agreement may be directed to Sri Lanka Tour Pilot using the contact details published on SriLankaTourPilot.com.
